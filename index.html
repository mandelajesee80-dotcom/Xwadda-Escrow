How escrow works

Four steps from agreement to payout

Every deal follows the same order, so both sides know what comes next.

STEP 1

Agree the terms

Buyer and seller settle the item, the price, the delivery method and how long the buyer has to inspect it. We record it.

STEP 2

Buyer pays into escrow

The buyer sends the agreed amount to the escrow account shown for that deal, by M-Pesa or cryptocurrency. We confirm it arrived.

STEP 3

Seller delivers

Once we tell the seller the funds are held, they hand over the goods or complete the work. The buyer checks it against the agreed terms.

STEP 4

Release or dispute

The buyer confirms and we pay the seller, less our fee. If something is wrong, the buyer raises a dispute and we review it before any money moves.

How to tell the real Xwadda Escrow from a copy

Fake escrow sites are a common scam. Check these before you send money to anyone, including us.

What we do

Show the escrow payment details on your deal page at xwaddaescrow.co.ke.

Confirm to both sides when funds are received.

Release money only after the buyer confirms or a dispute is settled.

What we never do

Ask for your M-Pesa PIN or your bank password.

Ask you to pay to any number that is not shown on your deal page or kiosk order page at xwaddaescrow.co.ke.

Ask you to pay by gift card or airtime, or to any crypto wallet that is not shown on your deal page.

Release funds because a seller or buyer says we should, without the steps above.
